# ReflexML global novelty audit — 2026-09-29

## Provenance and scope

This document records the final project-level global salvage-audit decision adopted at the ReflexML freeze. A standalone detailed final global-audit report was not preserved as a repository artifact; the authoritative final snapshot and research postmortem preserve the resulting project-level decision.

The earlier narrow novelty audit remains historical evidence, but any recommendation to continue experimentation from that earlier stage is superseded by the final freeze. This document adds no new literature search, experiment, or scientific analysis.

## Global verdict

**NONE — NO DEFENSIBLE PAPER POINT AT ACCEPTABLE MARGINAL COST**

## Final project classification

**ONLY AS A REPLICATION / CONTROLLED EMPIRICAL STUDY**

## Final project decision

**FREEZE AND WRITE UP AS A CONTROLLED STUDY**

## Strongest surviving empirical results

Phase 5A reproduced a strong h1–3 gap and near-complete h4 attenuation. Same-S17 LR switching established a controlled endpoint LR effect. That contrast survived inherited-momentum Reset and was attenuated but not eliminated under the composite m=4 intervention. Historical exact replay localized closure at fixed probes. Wait-d found reunification-linked attenuation over tested d=1–4. MNIST remained AMBIGUOUS and Phase 5B remained insufficiently precise. These are scoped findings, not universal laws or a unique mechanism.

## Rejected candidate paper points

Generic rapid improvement after LR reduction, delayed catch-up, training-history forgetting, a new optimizer or causal method, a reliable rho-to-tau predictor, unique momentum/noise explanations, terminal-step novelty, and a Wait-d timing law do not survive the frozen evidence and contribution ceiling. The exact experimental combination and replay rigor do not independently establish paper-level novelty.

## Closest prior-art categories

The retained narrow audit covers LR transition/reconvergence, WSD/cooldown loss dynamics, delayed large-batch catch-up, LR/batch/noise relationships, finite-LR momentum dynamics and short-horizon optimization bias. Its near-neighbors include Hop, skip, jump to Convergence, Understanding WSD, and Fast Catch-Up, Late Switching. This provenance note adds no citations beyond the retained audit.

## Why no candidate justified further experimentation

Local information gain would not resolve the broad prior-art overlap, weak state-level measurability, bounded mechanism constraints or ambiguous external transfer at acceptable marginal cost. Neither additional replicas nor a more specific curve description automatically supplies a meaningful contribution.

## Why the project stopped

The stopping reason is the contribution/value ceiling, not compute unavailability or refutation of every hypothesis. Preserve reproducible effects, negative and unresolved results, frozen protocols and explicit claim limits. No further experiments are authorized under the current question.
